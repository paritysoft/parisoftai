# Content requirements — what's still needed before launch

The site is built to show nothing it can't back up. These items need real business input. Everything below is editable in the admin; no code changes are required.

## Blocking for launch

| Item | Where | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Public contact email (and optionally phone / location) | Settings → General | Empty — hidden on the site until set |
| Notification recipients for new inquiries | Settings → Notifications | Empty — falls back to `CONTACT_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL` |
| Privacy Policy reviewed by a legal professional | Pages → Privacy Policy | Draft template with a visible "draft" notice |
| Terms of Use reviewed by a legal professional | Pages → Terms | Draft template with a visible "draft" notice |
| Confirm budget ranges shown on the contact form | Settings → Contact form | Placeholder ranges — confirm or remove |
| Confirm the data-retention period (default 730 days) matches the Privacy Policy | Settings → Contact form | Needs decision |

## Strongly recommended

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Products** — names, one-line descriptions, platforms, verified store links, icons (≥256×256), screenshots | The Products section and pages stay hidden until at least one is published |
| **Portfolio** — 3–4 projects you have permission to show, with cover images, platforms, stack, challenge/solution | Client work needs written permission; otherwise show owned products or anonymised work with an attribution note |
| **Statistics** — confirm scope and period for "24.2K total users" and "200+ active subscribers" | Stored but hidden (Verified = off). Decide: which products, which period, measured how |
| **Founder figures** — "10+ years" and "50+ applications" are shown and labeled *Founder's experience* | Confirm wording is accurate; untick Verified to hide |
| **Testimonials** — only genuine quotes with written approval | Until then the section shows factual highlights |
| **About page story** — founder name/photo (optional), company registration details if desired | Current copy avoids attributing founder history to the new company |
| **Social profiles** (LinkedIn, GitHub, X…) | Settings → General |
| **Logo** — current mark is an original SVG built for this project; replace if you have a final brand logo | Settings → General → Logo |
| **Open Graph image** — a generated one is included; a designed 1200×630 image is better | Settings → SEO defaults |

## Copy notes

- Service page copy was written for this site from the capabilities you listed. Please review technical claims (e.g. specific frameworks like WinUI/.NET, Core ML) and remove any you don't want to offer.
- "Usually within two business days" reply time appears on the contact page — change it if that's not a commitment you want to make (Pages → Contact).
